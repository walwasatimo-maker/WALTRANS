1 · Choose languages

Tap the From and To boxes and search for any language. Choose Detect language and WalTrans works out what you typed, even in mixed sentences.

2 · Type, speak or scan

Type your text, tap the 🎤 to speak, or tap the camera to take a photo, pick a file or upload a recorded audio file. WalTrans reads the text, finds its language and translates it into your language.

3 · Translate

Tap Translate. Open ＋ Add context (market, formal, respectful…) to get wording that fits the situation.

4 · Understand and improve

  • 💡 Explain tells you what a word or sentence means and how it is used.
  • ✎ Correct teaches WalTrans the right translation. It remembers it, even offline.
  • The Chat tab lets two people talk in two languages.

  • Connect your free Sunbird AI account in Settings for all the Ugandan local languages e.g Lusoga, Luganda, e.t.c, context and voice in these languages.
  • Download the Offline pack on Wi-Fi to translate without internet.
WalTrans
Version 4.4 · Developed by Walwasa Timothy

WalTrans is a language translator built by a Ugandan University student for Uganda, Africa and the world at large. It understands all the Ugandan local languages e.g Luganda, Lusoga, Runyankore, Kiswahili, e.t.c and MOST of the languages in the world. It handles sentences that mix languages, explains words, reads text from photos and works both online and offline.

Built with

  • Sunbird AI (Sunflower) for Ugandan and African languages, voice and explanations.
  • Meta NLLB-200 for the offline pack (non-commercial licence).
  • Microsoft Translator and Cloudflare AI (through the app’s small free translation service) and MyMemory as online fallbacks.
  • Tesseract.js and PDF.js for reading photos and files.
  • Some Lusoga words from Wikipedia’s “Soga language” page.

Please remember

Machine translation can be wrong, especially for smaller languages. Don't rely on it for medical, legal or other critical decisions. Use ✎ Correct to help improve it.

In short

WalTrans has no accounts of its own, no ads and does not track what you do. The only thing it counts is anonymous usage numbers for the developer, which you can switch off (explained below). What you type, say or scan stays on your device, except when it has to be sent to a translation service to get your result (explained below).

Stored on your device only

Your history, saved corrections and translation memory, your own phrasebook entries, your settings, and (if you connect Sunbird AI) your Sunbird access token. Your Sunbird password is never stored. Delete everything any time in Settings → Your data, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

What is sent over the internet (only when you use it while online)

  • Text you translate or ask to explain goes to Sunbird AI (api.sunbird.ai) when you are connected, otherwise to the app’s free translation service (run by the developer on Cloudflare, which passes it on to Microsoft Translator or Cloudflare AI) or to MyMemory (api.mymemory.translated.net). These services receive your text and the language names, and have their own privacy policies. Please don't enter passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive details.
  • Speech: for Luganda, Lusoga and other languages your recording is sent to Sunbird AI to be turned into text. If you use your browser’s own voice recognition (for example for English), your browser company processes the audio under its own policy.
  • Audio files you upload are turned into text by Sunbird AI (when you are connected and the language is Luganda, Lusoga or another African language) or by the app’s free online service (Cloudflare speech recognition, run by the developer). The recording is sent in short pieces to do this and is not kept by WalTrans; it is not saved in your history. Delete it any time with the Delete button.
  • Photos and files are read on your device by open-source software (Tesseract.js, PDF.js); the picture itself is not uploaded. If you switch on “Use AI to fix reading mistakes”, the text that was read (not the picture) is sent to Sunbird AI. The first time you scan a language, the app downloads that language’s reading data from a public CDN. The text found is then translated like any other text (see above).
  • Offline pack: the model downloads from Hugging Face and runs on your device. Text translated with it never leaves your phone.
  • Code libraries load from public CDNs (cdn.jsdelivr.net, cdnjs.cloudflare.com). Like any website, they can see your IP address.
  • Anonymous usage counts (can be switched off, see below).
  • Feedback is sent only when you press Send. It contains your message, the optional contact you type, and, if ticked, basic app info.

Anonymous usage counts

So the developer can tell whether the app is really being used, WalTrans sends a few plain numbers to the developer’s own counting page: a random ID made on your device (not linked to your name, phone number or accounts), the app version, your device type (for example Android), whether WalTrans is installed on your home screen, and simple counts such as “app opened”, “a translation was made” and “a document was scanned”. Your country is worked out by the server from your connection; your network address is not saved. Nothing you type, say, scan or translate is ever included. The random ID is new if you clear the app’s data. Turn this off any time in Settings → Preferences.

Your choices

You can use WalTrans fully offline (phrasebook and offline pack) so nothing leaves your device, switch off the anonymous usage counts, disconnect Sunbird AI at any time, and delete your data in Settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. For questions or requests, use Settings → Send feedback.

Take a photo, or choose photos, a PDF, a Word (.docx), a text file or a recorded audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV…) with the same button. WalTrans writes down what it reads exactly as it appears (same lines and layout), shows the language it found in the “From” box, then translates into the language you choose.

For audio, WalTrans listens, writes down what was said, finds the language and translates it. For a recording, it uses the first 5 minutes (up to 15 MB).
